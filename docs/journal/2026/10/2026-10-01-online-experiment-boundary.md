# Online Experiment boundary

Date: 2026-10-01

Textus Corpus remains the immutable evaluation-input authority for reproducible offline experiments. CNCF Phase 98 and Textus Experiment may additionally support online A/B experiments driven by real application requests.

Online assignment does not require each production request to become a CorpusCase and does not make Corpus a traffic router. Corpus may later receive reviewed/sanitized production-derived cases through an explicit curation flow, but that is separate from online Experiment assignment and observation.
